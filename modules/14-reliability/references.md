# Module 14 References

## SRE Foundations

- [Site Reliability Engineering (Google)](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) — the canonical SRE reference; Chapters 4 (SLOs), 13 (Emergency Response), 14 (Managing Incidents) are most relevant
- [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/) — practical implementation guidance; Chapter 2 (Implementing SLOs) is essential
- [SLO burn rate alerting](https://sre.google/workbook/alerting-on-slos/) — Google's burn rate alerting methodology for error budgets

## Resilience Patterns

- [Release It! (Michael Nygard)](https://pragprog.com/titles/mnee2/release-it-second-edition/) — the foundational text on stability patterns including circuit breakers, bulkheads, and timeouts
- [Patterns for Resilient Applications](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/exponential-backoff-and-jitter/) — AWS guide on exponential backoff and jitter
- [Circuit Breaker Pattern (Martin Fowler)](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/CircuitBreaker.html) — original articulation of the pattern

## AI-Specific Reliability

- [Reliable Machine Learning (O'Reilly)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/reliable-machine-learning/9781098106218/) — reliability engineering for ML systems
- [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/) — curated repository of real AI system failures and incidents

## Load Shedding

- [Load Shedding in Distributed Systems](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/using-load-shedding-to-avoid-overload/) — AWS guide on load shedding strategies
- [Backpressure in Reactive Systems](https://www.reactive-manifesto.org/) — reactive manifesto's framing of backpressure

## Incident Management

- [PagerDuty Incident Response Documentation](https://response.pagerduty.com/) — practical incident response runbook templates
- [Postmortems (Etsy)](https://github.com/etsy/opsweekly/wiki/Postmortems) — blameless postmortem culture
- [Google's Postmortem Philosophy](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/) — SRE Book chapter on postmortems

## Disaster Recovery

- [Disaster Recovery Planning for AI Systems](https://cloud.google.com/architecture/dr-scenarios-planning-guide) — GCP DR planning guide (applicable principles)
- [Kubernetes Backup with Velero](https://velero.io/) — Kubernetes resource and volume backup
